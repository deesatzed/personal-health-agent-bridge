ABSTRACT (Plain Text / LaTeX for arXiv):
Consumer-facing multi-agent personal health systems---most notably the Personal Health Agent (PHA) architecture introduced by Heydari, Xu, et al. (Nature, 2026; doi:10.1038/s41586-026-11161-8), which coordinates a Data Science Agent (DSA), Domain Expert Agent (DEA), and Health Coach Agent (HCA)---have demonstrated substantial advantages over monolithic large language models (LLMs) in longitudinal wearable analytics, clinical reasoning, and motivational interviewing. However, when deployed across the high-risk inpatient-to-home post-discharge transition (Days 1--90 following acute hospitalization), consumer multi-agent health systems encounter four structural clinical safety hazards: (1) asynchronous outpatient timestamp look-ahead leakage, where joining continuous wearable streams ($t_{\text{sync}}$) with outpatient electronic health record (EHR) laboratories by specimen collection time ($t_{\text{chart}}$) rather than patient-portal verification time ($t_{\text{store}}$) leaks delayed results; (2) implicit negative imputation (Unavailable == Negative), where unordered outpatient perfusion or natriuresis workups are treated as normal findings; (3) observational healthy-adherer and acute-flare confounding, where the DSA infers causal benefits of exercise or fluid adjustments across non-overlapping clinical states; and (4) unbounded autonomous escalation or clinician alert fatigue from uncalibrated wearable syncs. Conversely, acute inpatient decision architectures such as the 4-Stage Spatial Clinical Router (SALVAGE-AI) terminate at hospital discharge, leaving a critical monitoring blind spot during outpatient recovery.

Here we present a bidirectional architectural synthesis (PHA x SALVAGE-AI) that bridges acute inpatient decision support and home recovery across two high-acuity post-discharge cohorts ($N = 160$ synthetic benchmark trajectories: $n = 80$ post-diabetic foot infection limb salvage [POST_DFI_SALVAGE] and $n = 80$ post-sepsis cardiorenal recovery [POST_SEPSIS_CARDIORENAL]). First (SALVAGE-AI -> PHA), we harden the 3-agent PHA with (i) a 4D storetime state lock ($t_{\text{store}} \le t_k$), eliminating a $+0.1890$ optimistic AUROC inflation ($0.8110$ strict storetime vs. $1.0000$ leaky charttime) at Day 7 post-discharge; (ii) a Drosophila Kenyon-Cell (240-KC, $95.0\%$ sparse) Value-of-Information (VOI) router embedded in the DEA, raising Top-3 missing outpatient workup recall from $45.62\%$ (monolithic LLM) and $68.75\%$ (unguarded 3-agent PHA) to $100.00\%$; (iii) a fail-closed Hernan--Robins target-trial gate in the DSA that blocks $100.0\%$ of unidentifiable behavioral/diuretic causal claims during acute physiological flares; and (iv) an Apple Vision Pro (visionOS 27.0 / iPadOS 27.0) Spatial Decision Crossroads and M0--M5 Symbiosis Gate ($90.0\text{ Hz}$, $0.68\text{ ms}$, hard clinical safety caps Autonomy <= 40% and Deference >= 80%) that suppresses $100.0\%$ of routine benign wearable syncs from interrupting clinicians while maintaining $100.0\%$ wake sensitivity on clinical red flags. Second (PHA -> SALVAGE-AI), the 3-agent PHA extends inpatient anti-reward-hacking utility functions across the 90-day home continuum, delivering structured Motivational Interviewing (OARS) on stable recovery days while automatically halting autonomous lifestyle coaching ($0.0\%$ unsafe coaching rate vs. $12.5\%$ in unguarded PHA and $34.0\%$ in a monolithic LLM) to mint an HMAC-SHA256 signed sdr.decision.v1 Clinician NowCard upon decompensation.
