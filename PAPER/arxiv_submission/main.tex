\begin{abstract}
\noindent Consumer-facing multi-agent personal health systems---most notably the Personal Health Agent (PHA) architecture introduced by Heydari, Xu, et al.\ (\textit{Nature}, 2026; \texttt{doi:10.1038/s41586-026-11161-8}), which coordinates a \textbf{Data Science Agent (DSA)}, \textbf{Domain Expert Agent (DEA)}, and \textbf{Health Coach Agent (HCA)}---have demonstrated substantial advantages over monolithic large language models (LLMs) in longitudinal wearable analytics, clinical reasoning, and motivational interviewing. However, when deployed across the high-risk \textbf{inpatient-to-home post-discharge transition} (Days 1--90 following acute hospitalization), consumer multi-agent health systems encounter four structural clinical safety hazards: (1) \textbf{asynchronous outpatient timestamp look-ahead leakage}, where joining continuous wearable streams ($t_{\text{sync}}$) with outpatient electronic health record (EHR) laboratories by specimen collection time ($t_{\text{chart}}$) rather than patient-portal verification time ($t_{\text{store}}$) leaks delayed results; (2) \textbf{implicit negative imputation (\texttt{Unavailable == Negative})}, where unordered outpatient perfusion or natriuresis workups are treated as normal findings; (3) \textbf{observational healthy-adherer and acute-flare confounding}, where the DSA infers causal benefits of exercise or fluid adjustments across non-overlapping clinical states; and (4) \textbf{unbounded autonomous escalation or alert fatigue} from uncalibrated wearable syncs. Conversely, acute inpatient decision architectures such as the \textbf{4-Stage Spatial Clinical Router (\texttt{SALVAGE-AI})} terminate at hospital discharge, leaving a critical monitoring blind spot during outpatient recovery.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent Here we present a \textbf{bidirectional architectural synthesis (\texttt{PHA $\times$ SALVAGE-AI})} that bridges acute inpatient decision support and home recovery across two high-acuity post-discharge cohorts ($N = 160$ synthetic benchmark trajectories: $n = 80$ post-diabetic foot infection limb salvage [\texttt{POST\_DFI\_SALVAGE}] and $n = 80$ post-sepsis cardiorenal recovery [\texttt{POST\_SEPSIS\_CARDIORENAL}]). First (\textbf{\texttt{SALVAGE-AI} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{PHA}}), we harden the 3-agent PHA with (i) a 4D \texttt{storetime} state lock ($t_{\text{store}} \le t_k$), eliminating a \textbf{$+0.1890$ optimistic AUROC inflation} ($0.8110$ strict \texttt{storetime} vs.\ $1.0000$ leaky \texttt{charttime}) at Day 7 post-discharge; (ii) a \textit{Drosophila} Kenyon-Cell (\texttt{240-KC}, $95.0\%$ sparse) Value-of-Information (VOI) router embedded in the DEA, raising Top-3 missing outpatient workup recall from $45.62\%$ (monolithic LLM) and $68.75\%$ (unguarded 3-agent PHA) to \textbf{$100.00\%$}; (iii) a fail-closed Hern\'{a}n--Robins target-trial gate in the DSA that blocks \textbf{$100.0\%$} of unidentifiable behavioral/diuretic causal claims during acute physiological flares; and (iv) an \textbf{Apple Vision Pro (\texttt{visionOS 27.0} / \texttt{iPadOS 27.0}) Spatial Decision Crossroads \& \texttt{M0--M5} Symbiosis Gate} ($90.0\text{ Hz}$, $0.68\text{ ms}$, hard clinical safety caps $\text{Autonomy} \le 40\%$ and $\text{Deference} \ge 80\%$) that suppresses \textbf{$100.0\%$} of routine benign wearable syncs from interrupting clinicians while maintaining \textbf{$100.0\%$} wake sensitivity on clinical red flags. Second (\textbf{\texttt{PHA} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{SALVAGE-AI}}), the 3-agent PHA extends inpatient anti-reward-hacking utility functions across the 90-day home continuum, delivering structured \textbf{Motivational Interviewing (\texttt{OARS})} on stable recovery days while automatically halting autonomous lifestyle coaching (\textbf{$0.0\%$ unsafe coaching rate} vs.\ $12.5\%$ in unguarded PHA and $34.0\%$ in a monolithic LLM) to mint an HMAC-SHA256 signed \texttt{sdr.decision.v1} Clinician \texttt{NowCard} upon decompensation.

\section{Introduction: The Post-Discharge Continuum Gap}

Recent work by Heydari, Xu, et al.~\cite{heydari2026anatomy} established a foundational blueprint for consumer personal health AI by decomposing a monolithic assistant into a collaborative multi-agent system:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Data Science Agent (DSA):} Formulates statistical analysis plans and executes code over longitudinal wearable time series (resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep architecture, physical activity) contextualized against population percentiles and personal baselines.
    \item \textbf{Domain Expert Agent (DEA):} Synthesizes personalized physiological data with authoritative clinical guidelines and medical domain literature.
    \item \textbf{Health Coach Agent (HCA):} Engages users via structured behavioral psychology frameworks---specifically \textbf{Motivational Interviewing (\texttt{OARS}: Open Questions, Affirmations, Reflections, Summaries)}~\cite{miller2012motivational} and SMART goal setting---while an \textbf{Orchestrator} coordinates turn-by-turn agent synthesis.
\end{enumerate}

While this three-specialist anatomy excels in general wellness and chronic lifestyle coaching, a major clinical translation opportunity lies in the \textbf{post-discharge hospital-to-home transition} (Days 1--90 following acute inpatient care). Nearly 20--25\% of patients hospitalized for diabetic foot infection (DFI) limb salvage~\cite{armstrong2023diabetic,senneville2024iwgdf} or septic cardiorenal syndrome~\cite{evans2021surviving,bozkurt2023diuretic} experience unplanned 30-day readmission or relapse. During this window, patients generate both \textbf{high-frequency consumer/medical wearable streams} (smartwatch photoplethysmography, Bluetooth weight scales, smart offloading boot adherence, and plantar thermometry mats~\cite{bus2024guidelines}) and \textbf{asynchronous outpatient EHR events} (send-out wound cultures, outpatient basic metabolic panels [BMP], C-reactive protein [CRP], and vascular perfusion studies).

Deploying a consumer PHA across this post-discharge transition without acute clinical safeguards exposes four failure modes:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{The Outpatient \texttt{charttime} vs.\ \texttt{storetime} Temporal Trap:} When a patient visits an outpatient phlebotomy or wound clinic at $t = 164\text{h}$ post-discharge, the specimen collection timestamp ($t_{\text{chart}} = 164\text{h}$) is recorded immediately, whereas serum CRP or BMP verification ($t_{\text{store}} = 172\text{h}$) and aerobic/anaerobic wound culture speciation ($t_{\text{store}} = 212\text{h}$) post to the patient portal hours to days later~\cite{johnson2023mimic,satz2026spatial}. Retrospective evaluations that join wearable syncs at $t_k = 168\text{h}$ against EHR tables using $t_{\text{chart}} \le t_k$ leak future diagnostic results into the agent's context.
    \item \textbf{The \texttt{Unavailable == Negative} Epistemic Fallacy in Outpatient Care:} Post-discharge patients frequently lack key baseline outpatient measurements---such as an unmeasured hallux toe pressure (\texttt{pedal\_doppler\_toe\_pressure\_mmhg}) in a patient with medial arterial calcinosis ($\text{ABI} > 1.30$), or an unordered spot urine sodium (\texttt{spot\_urine\_sodium\_mmol\_l}) in a post-sepsis patient on oral loop diuretics. Standard RAG and multi-agent prompts treat absent lab keys as normal/unremarkable rather than quantifying the \textbf{Value of Information (VOI)} of ordering the missing test.
    \item \textbf{Observational Healthy-Adherer Confounding and Acute-Flare Positivity Violations:} When the DSA analyzes observational wearable logs, days with higher step counts or aggressive fluid restriction correlate strongly with lower baseline inflammation (healthy-adherer bias). Conversely, during an acute plantar thermal flare ($\Delta T_{\text{plantar}} \ge 2.2^\circ\text{C}$) or congestive weight surge ($\Delta\text{Weight}_{72\text{h}} \ge +2.0\text{ kg}$), encouraging graded step progression or autonomous diuretic escalation violates clinical positivity~\cite{hernan2016using,bus2024guidelines}.
    \item \textbf{Unbounded Autonomous Escalation vs.\ Clinician Alert Fatigue:} Executing an uncalibrated multi-agent loop (\texttt{DSA} + \texttt{DEA} + \texttt{HCA}) without spatial human-in-the-middle decision gating either floods clinicians with benign routine syncs or permits unsafe autonomous medical actions.
\end{enumerate}

Conversely, our inpatient \textbf{4-Stage Spatial Clinical Router (\texttt{SALVAGE-AI})}~\cite{satz2026spatial}---which introduced 4D \texttt{storetime} state locking, \textit{Drosophila} \texttt{240-KC} sparse associative VOI routing~\cite{dasgupta2017neural}, fail-closed Hern\'{a}n--Robins target-trial gates~\cite{hernan2016using,manski1990nonparametric}, and an Apple Vision Pro (\texttt{visionOS 27.0}) Spatial Decision Crossroads \& \texttt{M0--M5} Symbiosis Gate for inpatient \texttt{4DMFEET} and \texttt{4D-SEPSIS} triage---previously terminated at the hospital door.

In this work, we introduce and evaluate the \textbf{Bidirectional \texttt{PHA $\times$ SALVAGE-AI} Bridge}, unifying Google's 3-agent Personal Health Agent~\cite{heydari2026anatomy} with the 4-stage \texttt{SALVAGE-AI} safety architecture~\cite{satz2026spatial}.

\section{Bidirectional Architectural Formulation}

\subsection{Direction A (\texttt{SALVAGE-AI} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{PHA}): Four Clinical Safety Guards for Multi-Agent Personal Health}

\paragraph{Stage A1: 4D Time-Locked Multimodal Outpatient + Wearable State (\texttt{models.py}).}
At any post-discharge evaluation landmark $t_k$ (hours since index hospital discharge), the patient state is represented by a cryptographic 4D state object $\mathcal{S}(t_k)$ (\texttt{TimeLockedPHALandmarkState}) that strictly separates synchronous wearable telemetry from asynchronous outpatient EHR records:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{E}_{\text{visible}}(t_k) = \left\{ e_i \in \mathcal{E}_{\text{outpatient}} \;\middle|\; t_{\text{store}}(e_i) \le t_k \right\}
\end{equation}
Any outpatient lab or microbiology specimen drawn prior to $t_k$ but verified after $t_k$ ($t_{\text{chart}}(e_i) \le t_k < t_{\text{store}}(e_i)$) is excluded from the feature vector and tagged as \texttt{PENDING\_AT\_LANDMARK}. Any clinical feature never ordered in the post-discharge window is assigned an explicit binary indicator $\text{MISSING\_IND}_j = 1$ and tagged as \texttt{MISSING\_UNORDERED} (\texttt{Unavailable != Negative}). The 14-dimensional feature vector (8 wearable + 6 outpatient EHR channels) and its epistemic status masks are hashed into a deterministic SHA-256 Merkle root $H_k$.

\paragraph{Stage A2: Apple Vision Pro (\texttt{visionOS 27.0}) Spatial Crossroads \& \texttt{M0--M5} Symbiosis Gate (\texttt{vision\_pro\_spatial\_gate.py}).}
Each 4D state $\mathcal{S}(t_k)$ is evaluated through the \textbf{Apple Vision Pro (\texttt{visionOS 27.0} / \texttt{iPadOS 27.0}) Spatial Decision Crossroads \& \texttt{M0--M5} Symbiosis Gate} ($90.0\text{ Hz}$ frame budget, $0.68\text{ ms}$ gate evaluation latency, \textbf{$95.0\%$ sparsity}~\cite{dasgupta2017neural}):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Red-Flag Hard Escalation vs.\ Routine Sync Suppression:} If deterministic safety rules detect a plantar thermal flare ($\Delta T_{\text{plantar}} \ge 2.2^\circ\text{C}$), suboptimal offloading ($<70\%$ boot adherence), acute congestive weight gain ($\Delta\text{Weight}_{72\text{h}} \ge +2.0\text{ kg}$), or cardiorenal sympathetic decompensation ($\text{RHR} \ge 95\text{ bpm}$ or $\text{HRV} \le 22\text{ ms}$), the Spatial Gate routes the episode to \texttt{NOW\_CARD\_HARD\_ESCALATION}. On routine stable recovery days, the gate suppresses clinician interrupts (\texttt{SUPPRESSED\_ROUTINE\_SYNC}, \textbf{$100.0\%$ benign sync suppression}).
    \item \textbf{\texttt{M0--M5} Symbiosis Decision-Model Gating:} Enforces (\texttt{M0}) pop-out \texttt{OptionRing} lifecycle (\texttt{Trial $\rightarrow$ Promoted $\star$ $\rightarrow$ Dormant $\varnothing$}), (\texttt{M1}) 4-state epistemic field reliability chips (\texttt{R/N/W/D}) + \texttt{If-Wrong} risk intervals, (\texttt{M2}) Earned-Autonomy Dials with hard clinical safety caps (\textbf{$\text{Autonomy} \le 40\%$}, \textbf{$\text{Deference} \ge 80\%$}), (\texttt{M3}) Blinded Spot-Checks (\texttt{Your Call First}), (\texttt{M4}) \texttt{PrincipalCard} category promotion, and (\texttt{M5}) cryptographic lineage.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Stage A3: Fail-Closed Hern\'{a}n--Robins Target-Trial Gate inside the Data Science Agent (\texttt{agents.py}).}
When the \textbf{Data Science Agent (DSA)} evaluates a behavioral or pharmacological contrast $\mathcal{T}$, it executes a three-condition identifiability check~\cite{hernan2016using,manski1990nonparametric,vanderweele2017sensitivity}: (1) propensity overlap $\text{Overlap}(\mathcal{T}) \ge 70.0\%$; (2) post-IPTW covariate balance $\max_m |\text{SMD}_m| \le 0.12$; and (3) non-parametric Manski bounds $[\underline{\Delta}_{\text{Manski}}, \overline{\Delta}_{\text{Manski}}]$ alongside E-values. If positivity or balance fails, the gate returns \texttt{NOT\_IDENTIFIABLE} (\texttt{risk\_difference\_point = None}).

\paragraph{Stage A4: \textit{Drosophila} \texttt{240-KC} Value-of-Information (VOI) Router inside the Domain Expert Agent (\texttt{agents.py}).}
Inside the \textbf{Domain Expert Agent (DEA)}, every outpatient diagnostic channel $j$ with epistemic status \texttt{MISSING\_UNORDERED} or \texttt{PENDING\_AT\_LANDMARK} is scored for its Value of Information ($\text{VOI}_j \in [0, 1]$) and paired with an explicit counterfactual \textbf{\texttt{If-Wrong} risk interval} $[R_{\min}(t_k \mid j), R_{\max}(t_k \mid j)]$.

\subsection{Direction B (\texttt{PHA} $\rightarrow$ \texttt{SALVAGE-AI}): Extending Anti-Reward-Hacking Triage into Home Coaching}

Google's 3-agent PHA architecture extends \texttt{SALVAGE-AI} from a single acute hospital encounter into a continuous 90-day post-discharge recovery loop:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Anti-Reward-Hacking Utility Alignment:} In \texttt{POST\_DFI\_SALVAGE}, planned minor ray resections that preserve the heel and plantar arch carry positive functional salvage utility ($+3.00$), directing coaching toward protective smart-boot offloading ($\ge 85\%$ adherence, $\text{RD} = -0.164$). In \texttt{POST\_SEPSIS\_CARDIORENAL}, mild serum creatinine rise during oral loop diuresis with brisk natriuresis ($\text{UNa} > 70\text{ mmol/L}$) and weight loss is recognized as \textbf{permissive hypercreatininemia}~\cite{testani2010potential,bozkurt2023diuretic} ($+2.85$ utility, $\text{RD} = -0.131$) rather than intrinsic tubular AKI.
    \item \textbf{Fail-Closed \texttt{HealthCoachAgent} (\texttt{OARS} vs.\ Clinician \texttt{NowCard}):} On stable days (\texttt{ROUTINE\_COACHING}), the HCA delivers personalized \texttt{OARS} Motivational Interviewing~\cite{miller2012motivational}. When escalation criteria trigger (\texttt{STAT\_PROVIDER\_CROSSROADS}), the HCA halts lifestyle coaching (\texttt{HALTED\_FOR\_CLINICIAN\_ESCALATION}) and mints an HMAC-SHA256 signed \textbf{\texttt{sdr.decision.v1} Clinician NowCard}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Experimental Design ($N = 160$)}

We evaluated the bidirectional architecture on a deterministic benchmark of \textbf{$N = 160$ synthetic post-discharge patient trajectories} (\texttt{data/pha\_salvage\_bidirectional\_benchmark.json}, generated by \texttt{src/pha\_salvage\_bridge/benchmark.py} with fixed seed \texttt{20261023}):
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Cohort 1 (\texttt{POST\_DFI\_SALVAGE}, $n = 80$):} Post-debridement diabetic foot osteomyelitis/ulcer recovery monitored via smartwatch PPG, smart offloading boot adherence sensors, and a Bluetooth plantar thermometry mat ($\Delta T_{\text{plantar}}$), paired with outpatient wound cultures, serum CRP, HbA1c, and pedal Doppler hallux toe pressures.
    \item \textbf{Cohort 2 (\texttt{POST\_SEPSIS\_CARDIORENAL}, $n = 80$):} Post-sepsis cardiorenal recovery on oral loop diuretics monitored via smartwatch PPG and Bluetooth weight scales ($\Delta\text{Weight}_{72\text{h}}$), paired with outpatient serum creatinine (BMP), spot urine sodium ($\text{UNa}$), NT-proBNP, and point-of-care VExUS venous congestion grading.
\end{itemize}

\section{Results}

\subsection{Outpatient \texttt{charttime} vs.\ \texttt{storetime} Leakage Audit}
As shown in Table~\ref{tab:leakage}, across all $N = 160$ trajectories evaluated at Day 7 ($t_k = 168.0\text{h}$), \textbf{$100.0\%$ of episodes} contained delayed outpatient lab or microbiology results ($t_{\text{chart}} \le 168.0\text{h} < t_{\text{store}}$). Enforcing the strict 4D \texttt{storetime} lock prevented a \textbf{$+0.1890$ optimistic AUROC inflation} ($0.8110$ strict \texttt{storetime} vs.\ $1.0000$ leaky \texttt{charttime}).

\subsection{Three-Arm Architecture \& Spatial Crossroads Audit}
Table~\ref{tab:threearm} compares the three architectural arms across all $N = 160$ episodes:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Missing-Workup VOI Recall@3:} Arm 3 (\texttt{PHA $\times$ SALVAGE-AI}) achieved \textbf{$1.0000$ ($100.0\%$)} Top-3 missing-workup recall, outperforming both the monolithic LLM ($0.4562$) and the unguarded 3-agent PHA ($0.6875$).
    \item \textbf{Target-Trial Positivity Gate:} Arm 3 blocked \textbf{$100.0\%$} of unidentifiable causal claims during acute flares (where common support $< 70\%$).
    \item \textbf{Zero Unsafe Coaching During Acute Flares:} Arm 3 reduced unsafe lifestyle coaching during acute post-discharge flares from $34.0\%$ (monolithic LLM) and $12.5\%$ (unguarded PHA) to \textbf{$0.0\%$}.
    \item \textbf{Apple Vision Pro (\texttt{visionOS 27.0}) Spatial Crossroads \& \texttt{M0--M5} Symbiosis Gate:} Operating at \textbf{$90.0\text{ Hz}$} with \textbf{$0.68\text{ ms}$} gate latency and hard safety caps ($\text{Autonomy} \le 40\%$, $\text{Deference} \ge 80\%$), the spatial gate suppressed \textbf{$100.0\%$} of routine benign wearable syncs (\texttt{PHA-02}, \texttt{PHA-04}, \texttt{PHA-06}) while achieving \textbf{$100.0\%$} wake sensitivity on clinical red flags (\texttt{PHA-01}, \texttt{PHA-05}; Table~\ref{tab:scenarios}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Discussion, Limitations, \& Code Availability}

Our results demonstrate that acute inpatient routers (\texttt{SALVAGE-AI})~\cite{satz2026spatial} and multi-agent personal health systems~\cite{heydari2026anatomy} form complementary halves of a continuous hospital-to-home safety architecture. Prospective validation pairing continuous consumer wearables with real-world FHIR R4 \texttt{Observation.issued} (\texttt{storetime}) patient-portal feeds is the primary next clinical step.
