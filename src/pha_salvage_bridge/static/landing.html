1. Six Role-Specific Output Queues & Explicit Podiatry Visit Modalities

Role 1 · Home Health RN Visit Queue

Rapid 2–12h in-home nursing dispatches: re-sealing leaking NPWT (Wound VAC) drapes within the 4-hour desiccation window (HDFU-05), STAT home blood/urine/ketone draws, 12-lead home ECGs, decongestive compression-safe elevation (HDFU-09), and nocturnal hypoglycemia basal insulin de-escalation (HDFU-10).

Role 2 · Visiting Wound Care Specialist (CWOCN / NP)

Specialized mobile bedside wound procedures: conservative sharp paring of hyperkeratotic periwound callus rings at home (HDFU-07), post-debridement Levine deep tissue swabs, CAMP graft surveillance, and portable handheld PPG Hallux Toe Pressure ($\text{TP}$) VOI checks when brachial ABI is calcified (HDFU-06).

Role 3 · Home IV Antibiotics (OPAT & ID Stewardship)

Manages PICC/Midline parenteral antimicrobials: immediately holds nephrotoxic IV Vancomycin during acute-on-chronic renal failure (HDFU-03, trough $26.4\text{ }\mu\text{g/mL}$, $\text{Cr } 2.9$, $\text{K}^+ 5.8$), executes OVIVA IV-to-oral osteomyelitis transitions to remove PICC lines early (HDFU-11), and holds SGLT2i during infection/ketonemia (HDFU-02).

Role 4 · Podiatry Queue (3 Explicit Visit Modalities)

Assigns the exact clinical setting needed:
• 4A Podiatry Home / Tele-Co-Visit for wheelchair/homebound patients (HDFU-07, HDFU-09).
• 4B Podiatry Office Visit ("Toe-and-Flow" 24–48h Clinic) for weight-bearing X-rays, sterile Probe-to-Bone, arterial duplex, or non-removable TCC-EZ casting (HDFU-06, HDFU-08).
• 4C Inpatient Surgical Consult for deep abscess I&D (HDFU-01).

Role 5 · Hospital / ED / Cardiology-Nephrology Admit

Bypasses outpatient delays for life- or limb-threatening emergencies: normothermic ascending foot sepsis (HDFU-01), occult urosepsis + SGLT2i euglycemic DKA (HDFU-02), silent NSTEMI on wearable ECG patch (HDFU-04), or refractory hyperkalemic CRF crisis.

Role 6 · Silent Remission & HCA OARS Coaching

Suppresses 100% of benign recovery syncs from interrupting clinicians (HDFU-12) while delivering non-judgmental Motivational Interviewing (OARS) to patients and family caregivers for smart-boot adherence, contralateral shoe-lift balancing, and custom footwear graduation.

2. High-Risk Multimorbidity Monitoring in the Post-Discharge Diabetic Foot Population

1. Normothermic Sepsis Differential

CKD and diabetes blunt classic fever ($>40\%$ normothermic). Detects early sepsis via stress CGM surges ($>260\text{ mg/dL}$), RHR jumps, and HRV collapse, while distinguishing Foot Sepsis (HDFU-01) from Non-Foot Sepsis (HDFU-02).

2. Occult UTI & SGLT2i Euglycemic DKA

Diabetic cystopathy + SGLT2i glycosuria trigger ascending urosepsis and euglycemic DKA ($\text{BHB } 2.6\text{ mmol/L}$ at $\text{BG } 204\text{ mg/dL}$). Quiet foot telemetry ($\Delta T +0.4^\circ\text{C}$) prevents unnecessary foot surgery (HDFU-02).

3. Silent CAD & Autonomic Neuropathy

Cardiac Autonomic Neuropathy masks angina in $>40\%$ of diabetic MIs. Pairs normoglycemic cold diaphoresis/dyspnea and orthostatic drops with wearable ECG patch ST-depression ($-1.8\text{ mm}$) to catch silent NSTEMI (HDFU-04).

4. CRF, OPAT AKI & Venous Edema

Separates OPAT Vancomycin nephrotoxic AKI-on-CRF ($\text{U}_{\text{Na}} 24$, $\text{K}^+ 5.8$, HDFU-03) from suture-splitting congestive edema ($+2.4\text{ kg}$) with benign permissive hypercreatininemia ($\text{U}_{\text{Na}} 84$, HDFU-09).
